## Replacement description (English)

**Zuhause am Bach – Wachau | Private guest room for cyclists and hikers**

Stay in Aggsbach Markt on the northern bank of the Danube in the Wachau, between Emmersdorf and Dürnstein. Zuhause am Bach offers **one private double room (“Gartenzimmer”) for up to two guests**. It is **not an entire apartment or house**.

A convenient stop for guests cycling the **Danube Cycle Path (Donauradweg)** or hiking the **Wachau World Heritage Trail (Welterbesteig)**. One-night stays are generally possible, subject to availability.

Guest amenities include free Wi-Fi, free parking, a **lockable bicycle storage room with camera monitoring**, e-bike charging and access to the garden. Breakfast is available on advance request for an additional charge. Pets are not permitted.

The Gartenzimmer has an exclusive private bathroom directly across the hallway, outside the room.

**Check dates, current rates and book directly on the official website:** https://www.zuhauseambach-wachau.at/#booking
